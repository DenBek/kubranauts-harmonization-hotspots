The five colors are fixed bins, so the same color means the same kind of departure in every region. Switching the number between harmonized, MODIS, and VIIRS does not recolor the cell. That toggle is there to show the seam: the two sensors do not count fires the same way, and VIIRS is empty before April 2018.
