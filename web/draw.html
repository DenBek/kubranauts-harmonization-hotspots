Your own area

Draw an area, get its calendar

Draw a rectangle or a shape on the map of Argentina. We add up the fire detections of every map cell whose centre falls inside it and show the same calendar as the regions page, with a reliability flag for your area.

Examples

Choose a tool, then drag on the map (rectangle) or click corners and press Finish (polygon). The map cells are about 1 degree (roughly 100 km) wide.

Resolution limit: the data is summed over 1-degree cells (about 100 km). A shape smaller than one cell uses the whole cell. Before 2012 each month carries a reliability flag from the number of MODIS detections in the cell, so small or quiet areas are flagged, not hidden.

Calendar for your area

What to notice: the same colours as the regions page, computed for your shape. Usual = median of the same calendar month over 2003 to 2022 in your area. Hatched squares before 2012 are converted from MODIS, and their flag depends on how many MODIS detections your area has.

Series for your area

What to notice: the shaded band before 2012 is the likely range for your area's size. Small areas have wide bands.
